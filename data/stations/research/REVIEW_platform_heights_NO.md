## Notes

- **Laser older than the station:** Drammen (2015 scan, rebuilt 2025), Ski (2014 scan, Follobanen 2022) and Moss (2020 scan, temporary layout during the new-station works): their laser levels are of the old layouts; use Bane NOR's table heights.
- **Under roofs/terminals:** Nationaltheatret (underground), Oslo lufthavn (under the terminal), and canopied islands at Asker (2/3) and Lillestrøm (3/4, 6/7) have no laser surface.
- **Unclassified decks:** the 2012–2015 scans (Bærum, Asker, Romerike, Drammen, Follo, NDH Akershus) leave platform decks in class 1; levels marked `surface_from_class1` come from a flat class-1 band.
- **Oslo S (thinned 2019 data, ~1 pt/m²):** tracks 7–14 read 0.8–1.0 m above the bed against 570/700 mm; the bed there may be slab track, so the ballast-to-rail step can differ from the playbook's 0.15–0.2 m.
