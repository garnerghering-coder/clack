# Photo check: platform heights, laser vs register (2026-10-30)

Evidence only, no values decided. Inputs: `handoff/root/REVIEW_platform_heights_{SE,NO,FI,DK}.md` and each station's `photos.json`, `_report.json`, `README.md` and `station_facts.json`.

## Read this first: no image was actually looked at

This session could not open any Wikimedia image or file page:
- `commons.wikimedia.org` and `upload.wikimedia.org` are refused by the egress proxy (403 on CONNECT).
- WebFetch returns `url_not_found` for Commons pages, Commons API calls and thumbnails.
- Routes through a mirror and through the user's device were refused by the permission check, so they were not used.

What follows rests on the Commons metadata already in `photos.json` (title, description, date, author, licence, link). A few extra Commons category or file names came from WebSearch restricted to commons.wikimedia.org; those were not opened, so their dates and licences were not read. No Street View, Mapillary, maps, news or stock photos were used.

The verdict column therefore means:
- **supports register (by date)**: the metadata alone shows the laser scan predates a rebuild of that platform, so the laser reads the old layout. The edge height itself was not seen.
- **unclear (not viewed)**: a photo that probably shows the platform exists (best one listed), but the edge height vs the train step or rail could not be read. These are the photos a person should open first.
- **no usable photo**: nothing in the pack shows the platform. Where WebSearch found a Commons category, it is listed for a manual look.

How sure the platform/track identification is: **named** = the title or description names the track or platform; **station only** = the photo is at the station but no track is named; **weak** = probably not on the platform at all.

Scan dates: SE and DK from the review files; NO and FI from each `_report.json` `scan_date`.
