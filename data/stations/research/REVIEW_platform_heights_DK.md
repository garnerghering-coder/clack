## Pattern worth a look (not decided)

Most 50/55 cm platforms come out at 0.55–0.72 m with the playbook 0.175 m ballast-to-rail offset. A systematic +0.05…0.15 m could be a larger bed-to-rail-head offset on Danish track (concrete sleepers, deeper ballast) rather than wrong heights; only the rows above 0.125 m are listed. Esbjerg (Bilag 26 cm) measures 0.40–0.63 m: either the platforms were raised after the 1 August 2023 table or the outline/track match is off.
