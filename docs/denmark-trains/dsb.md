## DSB Vores Tog

DSB's subsidiary (based in Silkeborg) that took over GoCollective's (formerly Arriva's) regional trains on
**1 Sep 2026**, after the state ended GoCollective's contract in Sep 2025.
- 8 regional routes in Mid and West Jutland plus Funen: about 665 km and 117 stops. Includes Esbjerg–Niebüll,
  Aarhus–Herning–Struer and Odense–Svendborg.
- Fleet: about 43 Alstom Coradia LINT 41 and 15 Siemens Desiro DMUs (59 sets in total: 9 bought early, 50 at handover).
- Midtjyske Jernbaner runs the Thy line as a subcontractor from Oct (single source).
- Sources: dsb.dk/om-dsb/baredygtighed/virksomheden/vorestog/ ;
  dsb.dk/om-dsb/presse/pressemeddelelser/dsb-overtager-go-collectives-togtrafik-1.-september-2026/ ;
  railjournal.com/passenger/main-line/central-jutland-steps-in-to-help-dsb/

## Cross-border

- **Copenhagen–Hamburg EC:** DSB Talgo 230 sets with EB Vectron, run with DB. Reported to cover all departures from
  about May 2026; some Prague-linked runs use Czech ComfortJet (conflicting sources). Route via Fredericia/Padborg
  (the Fehmarn tunnel is not open; UNVERIFIED in this session). Sources: railwaypro, railjournal, seat61
  hamburg-to-copenhagen.
- **Øresundståg:** Swedish responsibility only since Dec 2022 (Skånetrafiken and the other Swedish regions, through
  Öresundståg AB). VR Sverige operates from Dec 2025. Trains still run Copenhagen H–Airport–Malmö. Kystbanen is run
  by DSB as a domestic service (from Dec 2022/2023; the sources differ). Sources:
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Øresundståg and /Kystbanen (snippets).
- **SJ X2000 Stockholm–Copenhagen:** Paused in 2024 for refurbishment. Due back in autumn 2026 (The Local,
  30 Mar 2026). Actual restart UNVERIFIED. Source: thelocal.dk/20260330/copenhagen-stockholm-high-speed-rail-connection-set-to-return
- **Snälltåget:** Daily Stockholm–Copenhagen since late Oct 2025. Daytime Stockholm–Malmö–Copenhagen–Hamburg since
  4 May 2026. Night train Stockholm–Hamburg–Berlin is being expanded for winter 2026/27. Source:
  mynewsdesk.com/se/snalltaget press releases.
- **Night trains via Copenhagen:** The state-procured Stockholm–Berlin night train (another operator, UNVERIFIED which)
  ends after summer 2026, according to Snälltåget.
