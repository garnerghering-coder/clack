## Lokaltog (Zealand, Lolland, Falster; part-owned by Movia/regions)
- 9 local railways, 338 km on Zealand, Lolland and Falster; formed 1 July 2015 (Lokalbanen + Regionstog).
  Src: https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Lokaltog (search summary, 2026-10-28)
- Lines (end stations from general knowledge, UNVERIFIED): Hornbækbanen Helsingør-Gilleleje; Gribskovbanen
  Hillerød-Gilleleje/Tisvildeleje; Frederiksværkbanen Hillerød-Frederiksværk; Nærumbanen Jægersborg-Nærum;
  Østbanen Køge-Hårlev-Fakse Ladeplads/Rødvig; Tølløsebanen Holbæk-Tølløse-Slagelse (UNVERIFIED end stations);
  Odsherredsbanen Holbæk-Nykøbing Sj.; Lollandsbanen Nykøbing F.-Nakskov.
- Lille Syd: since Dec 2020 the Køge-Roskilde part is served as an extension of Østbanen (Lokaltog, diesel);
  Roskilde-Ølby (~17 km) still not electrified per Feb 2025 Folketing letter; Køge-Næstved electrified March 2019.
  Src: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_South_Line (search summary); https://ft.dk/samling/20241/almdel/TRU/bilag/149/2981610.pdf
- Fleet: Alstom Coradia LINT 41 (41 sets total per 2018/19 Folketing doc; 12 sets for Østbanen/Køge-Roskilde).
  Src: https://ft.dk/samling/20181/almdel/TRU/bilag/126/1991447.pdf
- Region Hovedstaden lines: 25 LINT 41 + 4 Siemens-Duewag RegioSprinter (RegioSprinter only on Nærumbanen), c.2019.
  Src: https://edagsorden.regionh.dk/committee_1444/agenda_392929/documents/8fb53681-5cf3-4a3f-aed4-0f52a8eeb270.pdf
- IC2 (ex-DSB MR-type? UNVERIFIED origin; in service since 1997): 13 units (2021 Movia memo); run Lollandsbanen
  (photo evidence only, UNVERIFIED) and Tølløsebanen (UNVERIFIED).
  Src: https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Lokaltog ; Stadler PR below
- LINT 41 on Odsherredsbanen confirmed (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odsherred_Line, search summary).
- Upcoming: 14 Stadler FLIRT Akku 2-car battery units (+10 option), signed 8 Oct 2024, delivery 2027-2028, replace IC2;
  for Tølløsebanen and Østbanen, possibly Lollandsbanen and Odsherredsbanen.
  Src: https://stadlerrail.com/api/docs/0c9a08ae7e/202410_08_stadler-delivers-up-to-24-battery-powered-trains-to-lokaltog_en.pdf
- Nærumbanen: tender for 4 battery-electric sets to replace the 1999 RegioSprinters, service from late 2029-2030.
  Src: https://railwaynews.net/?p=249419 (Nov 2025)
- Two models on one line: Østbanen/Lille Syd (LINT 41, IC2 likely) and Tølløsebanen (UNVERIFIED which).

  Src: https://lex.dk/GoCollective ; https://www.altinget.dk/eu/artikel/dsb-overtager-gocollectives-togtrafik ;
